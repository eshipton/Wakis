# Chapter 3 · Diffraction and Scattering

Aperture diffraction, interference, and canonical 2D/3D Mie scattering validated against analytical theory, with comparisons of staircase, conformal, full-tensor, and body-fitted triangular discretizations.

## Tutorials

- **[Tutorial 3.1 · Single slit: Fresnel near field, Fraunhofer far field, and vectorial breakdown](03-01_single_slit/single_slit.ipynb)** ([`single_slit.py`](03-01_single_slit/single_slit.py)) — Plane-wave incidence, empty-domain source normalization, Fresnel number $N_F = a^2/(4\lambda L)$ near-field profiles vs 2D Rayleigh–Sommerfeld and Cornu spiral integrals, 2D Stratton–Chu near-to-far-field projection (`Near2FarMonitor`) vs Fraunhofer/Kirchhoff/Rayleigh–Sommerfeld obliquity factors, and vectorial breakdown at $a \sim \lambda$ ($\mathbf{E} \parallel$ vs $\mathbf{E} \perp$ slit edges, $\text{TE}_{10}$ waveguide cutoff at $a = \lambda/2$, and finite-thickness PEC screen vs zero-thickness sheet).
- **[Tutorial 3.2 · Double slit: Young's interference, missing orders, and inter-slit coupling](03-02_double_slit/double_slit.ipynb)** ([`double_slit.py`](03-02_double_slit/double_slit.py)) — Interference $\times$ diffraction envelope factorization ($\cos^2\alpha\,\operatorname{sinc}^2\beta$), angular fringe spacing $\Delta(\sin\theta) = \lambda/d$, missing orders $m = \pm p q$ for integer $d/a = p \in \{2, 3, 4\}$, polarization-dependent inter-slit surface-wave coupling for small separations, and forward pointer to Tutorial 7.5 (partially coherent double-slit interference).
- **[Tutorial 3.3 · N slits: Grating equation, $(N-2)$ secondary maxima, resolving power, and periodic vs finite-$N$ cost](03-03_n_slits/n_slits.ipynb)** ([`n_slits.py`](03-03_n_slits/n_slits.py)) — $N$-slit array factor $\left[\frac{\sin(N\alpha)}{N\sin\alpha}\right]^2 \operatorname{sinc}^2\beta$, principal maxima from $d\sin\theta_m = m\lambda$, $(N-2)$ secondary maxima and sidelobe suppression, $\Delta(\sin\theta) \propto 1/N$ peak narrowing, Rayleigh chromatic resolving power $R = \lambda/\Delta\lambda = |m|N$, and finite-$N$ vs $N \to \infty$ single-period (`DiffractionMonitor`) efficiency and cost comparison pointing forward to Chapter 6.
- **[Tutorial 3.4 · Mie scattering by a dielectric cylinder (2D)](03-04_mie_cylinder/mie_cylinder.ipynb)** ([`mie_cylinder.py`](03-04_mie_cylinder/mie_cylinder.py)) — Plane-wave scattering by an infinite cylinder ($n = 2$, $a = 1\;\mu\text{m}$, $x = 2\text{–}10$, TM and TE) with a total-field/scattered-field (`TFSFSource`) source, edge-level permittivity maps (staircase vs conformal vs full-tensor), body-fitted triangle meshes, and $Q_{\text{sca}}$ convergence against the exact Bessel series.
- **[Tutorial 3.5 · Mie scattering by a sphere (3D) and the near-to-far-field transform](03-05_mie_sphere/mie_sphere.ipynb)** ([`mie_sphere.py`](03-05_mie_sphere/mie_sphere.py)) — 3D Mie scattering of a lossless dielectric sphere ($n = 2$, $r = 1\;\mu\text{m}$, $x = 2\text{–}10$), near fields, 3D isosurfaces, and Stratton–Chu near-to-far-field projection (`Near2FarMonitor`) against Lorenz–Mie $S_1(\theta), S_2(\theta)$.
- **[Tutorial 3.6 · Localized surface plasmons of a metal cylinder: Drude–Lorentz pole count, body-fitted triangular vs Cartesian meshing, and cost per pole](03-06_plasmonic_cylinder/plasmonic_cylinder.ipynb)** ([`plasmonic_cylinder.py`](03-06_plasmonic_cylinder/plasmonic_cylinder.py)) — Localized surface-plasmon resonances (LSPR) of silver and gold nanocylinders ($2a = 40\;\text{nm}$ and $80\;\text{nm}$) across $300\text{–}900\;\text{nm}$ against the exact complex-index Mie series, separating Drude–Lorentz dispersion model error ($N = 1, 2, 3, 4, 6$ poles and Rakić 1998) from FIT discretization error on locally refined body-fitted triangular meshes (`UnstructuredFIT2D`) vs conformal Cartesian grids (`SolverFIT3D`), near-field $|\mathbf{E}|/|\mathbf{E}_{\text{inc}}|$ enhancement maps, and single-thread time/memory cost per pole.
