# Chapter 6 · Periodic Structures

Waveguide Bragg gratings, Bloch-periodic slow-wave structures, diffraction gratings, photonic crystals, and metasurfaces modeled with the Finite Integration Technique (FIT) in Wakis.

## Tutorials

- **[Tutorial 6.1 · Sidewall-corrugated waveguide Bragg grating](06-01_bragg_grating/bragg_grating.ipynb)** ([`bragg_grating.py`](06-01_bragg_grating/bragg_grating.py)) — 100-period sidewall-corrugated silicon waveguide Bragg grating ($\Lambda = 324\;\text{nm}$, $\Delta w = 50\;\text{nm}$): setup schematic, stopband reflection spectrum vs analytical coupled-mode theory (CMT), Gaussian apodization for sidelobe suppression, vernier misalignment destructive interference, and Kottke vs staircase and graded non-uniform sidewall-corrugation mesh convergence.
- **[Tutorial 6.2 · Slow-wave structure cold test: corrugated and glide-symmetric waveguides](06-02_sws_cold_test/sws_cold_test.ipynb)** ([`sws_cold_test.py`](06-02_sws_cold_test/sws_cold_test.py)) — Unit-cell Bloch-periodic eigenmode cold test of 2D parallel-plate and 3D rectangular corrugated and staggered-pillar slow-wave structures (mirror vs glide symmetry): setup and 3D isometric cut-plane schematics, Brillouin dispersion $\omega(\beta_0)$, group vs energy velocity, Floquet space-harmonic decomposition, Pierce interaction impedance $K_n$, and graded non-uniform vane-tooth/slot mesh comparison against multimodal field-matching and published benchmarks.
- **[Tutorial 6.3 · Quasi-bound states in the continuum: metasurface $Q \propto \alpha^{-2}$](06-03_quasi_bic_metasurface/quasi_bic_metasurface.ipynb)** ([`quasi_bic_metasurface.py`](06-03_quasi_bic_metasurface/quasi_bic_metasurface.py)) — Symmetry-protected bound states in the continuum (BICs) at $\Gamma$ in a sub-diffractive silicon bar-pair metasurface ([Koshelev *et al.*, PRL **121**, 193903, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.121.193903)): Fano resonance collapse at $\alpha = 0$, universal inverse-square scaling $Q_{\text{rad}}(\alpha) = Q_0\,\alpha^{-2}$ via `harminv`, `DiffractionMonitor`, and 1D RCWA, absorption $Q_{\text{tot}}$ saturation and bare $Q_{\text{rad}}$ recovery, and oblique-incidence angular dispersion $Q(k_\parallel) \propto k_\parallel^{-2}$ (`wakis.floquet`).
- **[Tutorial 6.4 · AR waveguide combiner: $k$-space map and grating order efficiencies](06-04_ar_waveguide_combiner/ar_waveguide_combiner.ipynb)** ([`ar_waveguide_combiner.py`](06-04_ar_waveguide_combiner/ar_waveguide_combiner.py)) — Diffractive augmented-reality waveguide combiner $k$-space closure ($\mathbf{G}_{\text{IC}} + \mathbf{G}_{\text{EPE}} + \mathbf{G}_{\text{OC}} = \mathbf{0}$), TIR field-of-view limits, achromatic $(m_R, m_G, m_B) = (4, 5, 6)$ multi-order momentum matching (Tian et al., *Light: Sci. Appl.* **14**, 94, 2025), and 2D oblique-incidence `wakis.floquet` in-/out-coupler sweeps and eye-box pupil expansion (Wang & Ma, `arXiv:2307.01600`, 2023) vs RCWA.
- **[Tutorial 6.5 · Slanted and blazed in-coupler gratings](06-05_slanted_grating_coupler/slanted_grating_coupler.ipynb)** ([`slanted_grating_coupler.py`](06-05_slanted_grating_coupler/slanted_grating_coupler.py)) — Unidirectional first-order coupling into the guided TIR mode for slanted, sawtooth blazed, and binary surface-relief gratings vs slant angle $\alpha$, groove depth $d$, fill factor $f$, and incidence angle $\theta_{\text{in}}$ (Wang & Ma, `arXiv:2307.01600`; Levola & Laakkonen, *Opt. Express* **15**, 2067, 2007), Kottke conformal subpixel vs staircase meshing, and multi-layer RCWA cross-checks.
- **[Tutorial 6.6 · Valley-Hall photonic crystal and domain-wall edge states](06-06_valley_hall_edge_states/valley_hall_edge_states.ipynb)** ([`valley_hall_edge_states.py`](06-06_valley_hall_edge_states/valley_hall_edge_states.py)) — Honeycomb photonic crystal on a centred-rectangular ($a \times \sqrt{3}a$) Cartesian FIT supercell (`wakis.lattices`): primitive-zone band unfolding, Dirac point at $K$ and valley-Hall bandgap opening under inversion-breaking sublattice asymmetry ([Ma & Shvets, *New J. Phys.* **18**, 025012, 2016](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/18/2/025012)), gauge-invariant Fukui–Hatsugai–Suzuki Berry curvature and valley Chern number sign reversal from PWE and 2D FIT fields, domain-wall and tuned trivial ribbon dispersion, and straight vs $120^\circ$ Z-bend flux ratio (bend over matched straight guide).
